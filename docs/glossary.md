# Glossary

**Venue**:          a physical location with a seating plan, made of sectors, rows and seats.
**Event**:          a performance (a concert, a show), possibly with several sessions, each with its own date, time and seat inventory.
**Price tier**:     a price category assigned to a set of seats in a session.
**Hold**:           a temporary reservation of a seat by a customer during checkout.
**Order**:          a confirmed purchase, associated with a payment.
**Ticket**:         issued for each seat of a paid order, and validated at the venue entrance.
**Organiser**:      publish events (venue, seating plan, price tiers, opening time of sales) and follow sales on a dashboard.
**Customer**:       browse the catalogue, hold seats, pay, and receive tickets.
**Gate staff**:     validate tickets at the venue entrance.
